Immersive Apprenticeships for Every Creative Mind

At our institute, apprenticeships aren't an add-on — they're built into your learning journey. Every student, across every course, steps into a paid, skill-aligned role within the heart of the creative and media industry.

Why Our Apprenticeship Model Stands Out

Direct Industry Integration

At our institute, students don’t just intern — they apprentice directly under working professionals in production houses, gaming studios, broadcast agencies, XR labs, PR firms, live event companies, and film sets.

Skill-Specific Placement

Every apprenticeship is tailored to your training and talent — whether you're stepping into a role as a 3D Game Artist, Media Planner, Sound Editor, AR/VR Assistant, Event Manager, Assistant Director, Makeup Lead, or Scriptwriter.

Paid Apprenticeship with Industry Exposure

We believe talent should be valued from Day 1. That’s why every apprenticeship is paid, offering not just hands-on experience, but fair recognition for your work.

Launchpad to Full-Time Roles & Freelance Careers

Many students are retained by their host organizations after apprenticeship. Others go on to build successful careers as independent creators, freelancers, or entrepreneurs in their fields.

Guaranteed Final-Semester Apprenticeships Across Creative Domains

All enrolled students are guaranteed an apprenticeship opportunity in their final semester — across sectors such as:

  • Film, TV & OTT
  • Gaming & XR
  • Sound & Audio Design
  • Makeup & Prosthetics
  • PR, Journalism &
    Brand Strategy
  • Digital Marketing &
    Content Creation
  • Live Events &
    Experiential Management
