About Us

Welcome to the Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) - where education is not textbook-deep. It is soul-deep.
This is where Padma Shri honorees, Oscar winners, National Award greats, global icons, and industry changemakers come together — not to lecture, but to share, shape, and spark the next generation of creators, communicators, and game-changers.
Whether your dream lies in acting and performing arts, journalism, filmmaking, sound design, music, event management, image strategisation, branding, digital storytelling, gaming, animation, vfx or beyond — Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) is where your ambition finds its roots… and wings.
Here, you're not just learning. You're becoming.

Our Vision

To be India’s foremost creative incubator —

where raw passion meets real-world mentorship, and aspiring creators evolve into industry-ready leaders.

At Indian Institute of Creative Skills, we envision a future where skills, stories, and self-expression converge to build the next generation of filmmakers, artists, journalists, performers, storytellers, and brand architects who redefine the global creative landscape — starting from Indian soil.

Our Mission

To nurture and empower young minds through hands-on, immersive learning rooted in industry realities

To provide access to mentors who are Padma Shri icons, Oscar winners, National Awardees, and globally respected voices across creative fields.

To foster a collaborative ecosystem where a journalist meets a brand custodian, a filmmaker meets a writer, singer, composer, where animators, vfx specialists, prosthetic makeup designers and game designers congregate, and together they co-create magic. To build a space where creativity is not a subject — it’s a lived experience. To prepare students not just for jobs, but for creative leadership, entrepreneurial thinking, and cultural impact.

Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship

MSDE Is Responsible For Coordination Of All Skill Development Efforts Across The Country, Removal Of Disconnect Between Demand And Supply Of Skilled Manpower, Building The Vocational And Technical Training Framework, Skill Up-Gradation, Building Of New Skills And Innovative Thinking Not Only For Existing Jobs But Also Jobs That Are To Be Created.

Skill India Mission

Launched in 2015, the Skill India Mission is a flagship initiative under MSDE aimed at training over 40 crore Indians in various industry-relevant skills by 2025. IICS is proud to contribute to this mission by creating high-impact learning experiences that turn raw talent into employable, entrepreneurial, and industry-ready professionals in the creative sector.

National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)

National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) as the ‘Principal Architect of the Skill Ecosystem’, stands as India’s leading force in skilling, reskilling, and upskilling, driving transformative initiatives that unlock opportunities for the workforce of tomorrow. By providing funding support, concessional loans, and innovative financial solutions, NSDC empowers enterprises, start-ups, and organizations to make a meaningful impact in emerging and futuristic skill sectors and empower India’s workforce to compete Globally.

Media & Entertainment Skills Council (MESC)

The Media and Entertainment Skills Council (MESC), established in 2012, is a Not-for-Profit Organization under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, dedicated to enhancing skill development in the Media and Entertainment sector. Initially funded by the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) and incubated at the Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FICCI), MESC operates as an awarding body with the National Council for Vocational and Education Trainings (NCVET).

Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS), India's only education incubator dedicated to the Media & Entertainment industry, we're not just teaching skills – we're launching careers. Backed by the NSDC Academy and powered by the Media & Entertainment Skills Council (MESC), IICS is your launchpad into the vibrant world of film, music, gaming, performing arts, and beyond.

The Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) is a private institution. It is affiliated with the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) Academy and operated by the Media and Entertainment Skills Council (MESC), which is funded by NSDC. While it receives funding and is affiliated with government bodies like NSDC and MESC, it is not a government-owned institution.

The Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) is located in Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi. The institute is situated on the 2nd and 3rd Floor, Plot No. 13A, Above Tata Motors Showroom, on Main Ring Road, in Vikram Vihar, Lajpat Nagar IV, New Delhi – 110024. ( 2nd and 3rd Floor, Plot No 13A, Ring Rd, Vikram Vihar, Lajpat Nagar 4, Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi, Delhi 110024 )

The Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) in Lajpat Nagar, Delhi, is open from Monday to Saturday, with hours of 10:00 am to 7:00 pm. IICS remains closed on Saturdays.

To apply to Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS), you can apply online through their website or offline by visiting the institute. The application process generally involves filling out the application form, attaching the required documents, and submitting the application. You may also need to appear for an entrance exam, such as the MECAT (Media & Entertainment Creative Aptitude Test).
