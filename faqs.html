Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS), India's only education incubator dedicated to the Media & Entertainment industry, we're not just teaching skills – we're launching careers. Backed by the NSDC Academy and powered by the Media & Entertainment Skills Council (MESC), IICS is your launchpad into the vibrant world of film, music, gaming, performing arts, and beyond.

The Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) is a private institution. It is affiliated with the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) Academy and operated by the Media and Entertainment Skills Council (MESC), which is funded by NSDC. While it receives funding and is affiliated with government bodies like NSDC and MESC, it is not a government-owned institution.

To apply to Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS), you can apply online through their website or offline by visiting the institute. The application process generally involves filling out the application form, attaching the required documents, and submitting the application. You may also need to appear for an entrance exam, such as the MECAT (Media & Entertainment Creative Aptitude Test).

We offer specialized degree programs in Bachelor of Design in Animation & Gaming, B.Sc. in Digital Content Creation & Media Management, B.Sc. in Sound Design & Music Video Production, BBA. in Event Planning & Management and certification courses like Hair, Makeup and Prosthetics, PR & Journalism, And Costume Design, each designed to equip you with industry-ready skills.

The admission journey includes an entrance exam, portfolio/audition review, and personal interview. We assess creativity, aptitude, and motivation to select candidates best suited for the course.

Yes, Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) does conduct an entrance exam for admission to its certificate programs. This exam is called the Media and Entertainment Creative Aptitude Test (MECAT).

We offer comprehensive career services including resume building, interview preparation, internships, apprenticeships with stipends, and direct recruitment opportunities through our industry partners.

Yes! All students get at least one fully funded international trip to global creative markets, festivals, or expos relevant to their course and performance.

We provide dedicated training, incubation support, mentorship, and resources for students looking to launch startups or creative ventures during and after their course.

Our campuses feature world-class creative labs, production studios, sound rooms, and collaborative workspaces equipped with the latest technology and software.

Absolutely. Our flexible class schedules and blended learning approach allow you to balance studies with professional or freelance work.

Through continuous industry collaborations, real-world projects, workshops by professionals, and participation in competitions, students build skills aligned with current market needs.
