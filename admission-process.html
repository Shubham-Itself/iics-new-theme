Admission Process

Admission Process

Apply Now

1. Online Entrance Exam

  • Tailored to your program
  • Tests creativity, aptitude, general awareness & industry interest
  • Conducted remotely and monitored

2. Portfolio Review / Audition

  • Creative Programs: Your portfolio shall be reviewed to assess your artistic vision, originality, and skill.
  • Performance Programs: You’ll be invited to submit or perform an audition task to showcase your talent.

3. Personal Interview

  • Shortlisted applicants will go through a one-on-one interview round (online or in-person).

4. Final Evaluation & Selection

  • Your performance across all stages — entrance test, portfolio/audition, and interview — is considered for final selection.
  • Selected students will receive a formal Offer Letter with details on admission, fee, and joining formalities.

Eligibility Criteria

Find the Right Course for Your Passion and Potential

Design in Animation & Gaming


✔ Successfully completed 12th grade (or equivalent) from a recognized board
✔ A strong interest or background in 3D modeling, animation, visual storytelling, or game design
✔ Creative thinking and a passion for digital arts and interactive media
No prior technical experience is required—just the curiosity to learn and the drive to create.

Digital Content Creation & Media Management


✔ Passed 12th grade (or equivalent) from a recognized board
✔ A strong interest in digital media, content creation, social media strategy, or visual storytelling
✔ A creative mindset and eagerness to explore the world of content, branding, and digital platforms
No prior experience needed—just passion, originality, and the willingness to turn ideas into impact.

Sound Design & Music Video Production


✔ Successfully completed 12th grade (or equivalent) from a recognized board
✔ A strong interest in music, audio production, sound design, or music video creation
✔ A creative ear, visual storytelling instincts, and a passion for blending sound with visuals
No prior training required—just a love for sound and a drive to create unforgettable audio-visual experiences.

Event Planning & Management


✔ Successfully completed 12th grade (or equivalent) from a recognized board
✔ A strong interest in event planning, brand activations, live entertainment, or hospitality
✔ Organizational skills, creative thinking, and a passion for creating memorable experiences
No prior experience needed—just the energy, vision, and drive to lead events that leave a lasting impact.

Hair, Makeup & Prosthetics


✔ Successfully completed 12th grade (or equivalent) from a recognized board.
✔ A strong interest in beauty, character creation, special effects, or performance arts.
✔ An eye for detail, steady hands, and a passion for transforming appearances through artistry.
No prior training needed—just creativity, curiosity, and a drive to master the art of visual transformation.

Costume Designing


✔ Successfully completed 12th grade (or equivalent) from a recognized board
✔ A keen interest in fashion, textiles, theatrical arts, or visual storytelling
✔ A creative eye for detail, love for character-driven design, and flair for fabric and form
No prior experience in fashion required—just a passion for design and a desire to bring characters to life through costume.

PR & Journalism


✔ Successfully completed 12th grade (or equivalent) from a recognized board
✔ A strong interest in media, communication, public relations, or storytelling
✔ Sharp observation skills, a curious mind, and a passion for words that inform, influence, and inspire.
No prior experience required—just a voice that wants to be heard and the drive to shape narratives that matter.
