Startup Support — Launch Your Creative Venture with Confidence

At IICS- Indian Institute of Creative skills, we believe creativity fuels innovation — and innovation sparks entrepreneurship. For students with bold ideas and entrepreneurial drive, our Startup Support Program provides the mentorship, resources, and industry access needed to turn your vision into a viable venture.
Whether you're starting a production house, launching a creative agency, building your own makeup brand, or developing a gaming studio — we’re here to back your journey from concept to company.

What We Offer

Incubation & Mentorship

Receive personalized guidance from leading founders, investors, and industry experts. Refine your ideas, build a strong business model, and navigate common startup challenges with confidence.

Workspace & Infrastructure

Kickstart your venture with access to cutting-edge creative labs, editing studios, production gear, rehearsal spaces, and co-working hubs — everything you need to operate and grow.

Pitching & Funding Opportunities

Take part in exclusive pitch days and funding rounds, in collaboration with angel investors, government initiatives, and startup accelerators tailored to the creative industry.

Workshops & Legal Support

From company registration to IP rights and branding — get expert guidance through hands-on bootcamps, legal advisory sessions, and business development workshops.

Collaborative Ecosystem

Become part of a thriving creative community — a dynamic network of artists, developers, strategists, and entrepreneurs. This is where future teams, partners, and co-founders are born.
