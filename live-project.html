Where Students Become Creators

Our LIVE Projects platform brings students from all programs together to collaborate on real-world, high-impact projects alongside industry mentors, media houses, production studios, brands, and agencies. This isn’t just practice — it’s professional work that reaches the public, with your name proudly attached.

What’s Unique About LIVE Projects

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

No matter if you’re a game artist, journalist, XR creator, event manager, sound designer, performer, media manager, or makeup artist—you’ll collaborate on full-scale, cross-functional projects designed to replicate real studio and agency workflows.

Credit-Worthy Portfolio Projects

Your work goes beyond the campus — projects are delivered directly to industry partners, featured on global platforms, showcased at festivals, and always credited to you.

Built-In Industry Exposure

These projects are an integral part of your academic journey, helping you build your showreel, strengthen your resume, expand your professional network, and gain real-world confidence—all before you graduate.
